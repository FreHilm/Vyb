Agents produce huge volumes of valuable reasoning that scrolls off
the terminal forever. Capturing the full transcript (input +
output) per agent session, browsable like the file-history view,
with markdown export → makes Vyb a research tool, not just a
runtime. Tie it to Kanban tasks ("show me everything Claude did
for T-033") and it's compounding value.
